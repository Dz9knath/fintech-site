FRAME 10 · FAQ

Questions

Is Reel 2 Real available yet?

No. This site is a prototype of the idea. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when there is something to try.

Does it work with Instagram?

For now it works with Instagram. TikTok integration is coming soon. The Reels on this page are drawn examples.

Are the prices real?

They’re estimates from a placeholder table, in US dollars. Real prices would be labelled live, with the time they were fetched.

Does it book anything?

No. The ticket in this prototype is a sample.

What happens to my photo?

It stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Do I need an account?

No. There’s no login on this site.
