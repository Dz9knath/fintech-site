About Reel 2 Real

We turn the Reels you save into trips you can actually take.

Our story

Most of us save Reels of places we would love to visit. A cliffside restaurant, a hidden beach, a street food market. They pile up in a saved folder, and almost none of them become a trip, because turning a clip into a plan means a dozen searches and a spreadsheet.

Reel 2 Real is our answer. You share a Reel to the app. It works out the place, shows an honest price for getting there, staying and eating, and keeps it on your own map until you are ready to go.

This page is a first draft of our story. We are still building the app, and the website is a prototype that shows how it will feel.
