What is Reel 2 Real?

An app that turns the Instagram Reels and TikToks you save into places on a map, each with an estimated trip price. This website is a prototype that shows how it will feel.

How does it find the place in a Reel?

In the finished app, AI will read the caption, tags and visuals, then show you its best guess and how sure it is. In this prototype the examples are scripted, so nothing here is real recognition yet.

Are the prices live?

No. Everything here is an illustrative estimate in US dollars from example data. In the app, each price will say whether it is live or estimated, and when it was fetched.

Does it work with Instagram and TikTok?

That is the plan: you share a Reel to Reel 2 Real from the share sheet. The connection is not live in this prototype.

Is it free?

There will be a free plan. The paid plans on this page are drafts and can change before launch.

Can I book flights and hotels?

Not directly. You will see the cost and the links out. Booking stays with the airlines and hotels.

When can I get in?

There is no launch date yet. Waitlist members hear first, and we will email only about launch.
